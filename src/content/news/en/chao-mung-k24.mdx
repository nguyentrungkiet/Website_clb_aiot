# Welcome Freshmen K24

Welcome, **K24 Freshmen**, to **Thu Dau Mot University (TDMU)** and specifically to the **AIOT Club** family! 🚀

## A New Journey Begins

Entering university is a significant milestone that opens up countless opportunities and challenges. At AIOT Club, we understand that the first days are always full of surprises. Therefore, this meeting is organized with the following purposes:
- Connecting freshmen with senior members.
- Introducing the club's development direction, vision, and mission.
- Sharing study experiences for specialized subjects (C/C++ Programming, Electronic Circuits, Artificial Intelligence).

## Highlights of the Meeting
1. **Networking & Chatting:** An opportunity to ask questions directly to Mentors and the Executive Board.
2. **Product Experience:** You will directly control Robots, watch Drone (UAV) demonstrations, and experience Smart Home systems developed by AIOT members.
3. **Mini-game:** Many attractive tech gifts are waiting for you.

> "Don't be afraid of being wrong, don't shy away from difficulties. Start from zero and create real products."

See you at the ISA Lab!
